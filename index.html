How to use in Blender
  1. Serve or open this page over HTTPS — Chrome on Android requires a secure context for motion sensors, so plain file:// may not trigger the permission prompt.
  2. Tap Enable sensors, point the phone the way you want "forward" to be, then tap Recenter.
  3. Tap Record, perform the move, tap it again to stop.
  4. Tap Export .py — it downloads to your phone.
  5. In Blender, open the Scripting tab, open the downloaded file, and click Run Script. A camera named "PhoneCam" is created with your take baked as keyframes.
